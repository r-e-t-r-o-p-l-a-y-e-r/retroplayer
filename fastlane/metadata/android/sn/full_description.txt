Retro Player inoridza mimhanzi nemabhuku ekuteerera zvakananga kubva pamaforodha ari pafoni yako. Inoshanda isina internet, haina kushambadzira uye haidi account.

Sei Retro Player?
• Inoenderera mberi chaiyo pawakasiya: album yega yega, audiobook uye playlist inochengetedza nzvimbo yayo uye kumhanya kwayo, kunyangwe mushure mekutangisa zvakare.
• Hapana kuskena raibhurari: hapana indekisi yekuvaka uye hapana chekumirira. Wedzera folda wotanga kuteerera; app inoverenga folda chete kana waivhura.
• Mafaira ako haabatiki: app inoona maforodha aunosarudza chete uye haimbochinji, haifambise kana kubvisa chero chinhu. Mafaira ainonyora chete ndiwo maplaylist aunochengeta.
• Anotaura mutauro wako: mitauro inopfuura 100, kubva kuArabic, Hindi neSwahili kusvika kuIndonesia, Vietnamese neZulu.

Yakagadzirirwa mabhuku ekuteerera nemaalbum
• Kumhanya kwekutamba kubva pa0.5× kusvika 2.0×, kunochengetwa pachinhu chimwe nechimwe; kusvetuka kwemasekonzi gumi nemashanu
• Zvitsauko zveM4B neM4A zviri pabhawa rekutsvaga, mune runyorwa rwezvitsauko uye muzviziviso
• Inodzokera shure kwemasekonzi mashoma kana ukaramba uchienderera mberi mushure mekuzorora kwenguva refu
• Kutamba kusina gapless; maalbum ane madisc akawanda anotamba nemadisc akarongwa

Maforodha ako, odha yako
• Tarisa semutariri wemafaira, uine breadcrumbs, zvinhu zvinofarirwa uye A–Z bar
• Sveta kuti uwedzere mafolda, maalbum kana matraki kumutsara uye uzvichengetedze seM3U playlist
• Inotamba nziyo dzekutamba dzawatova nadzo
• Ronga album yega yega nenhamba yenziyo, zita refaira kana zita remusoro

Kwese kwaunoteerera
• Zvinodzora zviri muchiziviso, pachikirini chekukiya (nekusvetuka kwemasekonzi gumi nemashanu) uye pamabhatani eBluetooth kana emota
• Android Auto: tsvaga raibhurari yako, zvaunofarira uye mutsetse uri mumotokari
• Widget yechidzitiro chepamba
• Zvingangodiwa kuti mota yako itange kutamba kana mahedhifoni kana mota yako yabatana
• Equalizer ine presets, chiedza uye rima theme

Mafomati: FLAC, M4A, M4B (AAC isina kunyorwa), MP3, OGG, Opus uye WAV.

Yakavanzika nekugadzira
Retro Player haina mvumo yeInternet, saka hapana chinobuda pafoni yako: hapana kushambadza, kuongorora, kutevedza kana maakaundi. Haidi mvumo yekuchengetedza futi.

Software yemahara uye yakavhurika (GPL-3.0-kana-yakazotevera). Gwaro remushandisi uye kodhi yekodha: https://github.com/r-e-t-r-o-p-l-a-y-e-r/retroplayer
