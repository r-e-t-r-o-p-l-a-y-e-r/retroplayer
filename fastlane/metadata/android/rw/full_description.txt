Retro Player icuranga umuziki n'ibitabo by'amajwi uhereye kuri dosiye ziri kuri telefoni yawe. Ikora hanze ya interineti, nta matangazo igira kandi nta konti ikeneye.

Kuki Retro Player?
• Ikomeza neza aho warekeye: buri alubumu, igitabo cy'amajwi na playlist bigumana aho biri n'umuvuduko wabyo, ndetse na nyuma yo kongera kuyikoresha.
• Nta gushakisha isomero: nta index yo kubaka kandi nta kintu cyo gutegereza. Ongeraho dosiye hanyuma utangire kumva; porogaramu isoma dosiye gusa iyo uyifunguye.
• Amadosiye yawe ntahinduka: porogaramu ibona gusa amadosiye wahisemo kandi ntiyigera ihindura, yimura cyangwa ngo isibe ikintu icyo ari cyo cyose. Amadosiye yandika ni urutonde rw'indirimbo wabika.
• Avuga ururimi rwawe: indimi zirenga 100, kuva ku Cyarabu, Igihindi n'Igiswayire kugeza kuri Indoneziya, Ikiviyetinamu n'Ikizulu.

Byakorewe ibitabo by'amajwi n'ama alubumu
• Umuvuduko wo gukina kuva kuri 0.5× kugeza kuri 2.0×, wabitswe kuri buri kintu; gusimbuka kw'amasegonda 15
• Ibice bya M4B na M4A biri ku murongo wo gushaka, ku rutonde rw'ibice no mu itangazo
• Isubira inyuma amasegonda make iyo ukomeje nyuma yo kuruhuka igihe kirekire
• Gukina nta mwanya; alubumu za disiki nyinshi zicurangirwa hakurikijwe urutonde rwa disiki

Amadosiye yawe, ibyo watumije
• Reba nk'umucunga dosiye, hamwe n'umugati w'imigati, ibyo ukunda n'akabari ka A–Z
• Kanda kuri 'stop' kugira ngo wongere amafolda, alubumu cyangwa indirimbo ku murongo hanyuma ubibike nk'urutonde rw'indirimbo rwa M3U
• Ikina urutonde rw'indirimbo usanzwe ufite
• Shyira kuri buri alubumu ukurikije inomero y'indirimbo, izina rya dosiye cyangwa umutwe wayo

Aho utega amatwi hose
• Igenzura mu itangazo, kuri ecran ifunze (hamwe n'amasegonda 15) no kuri Bluetooth cyangwa buto z'imodoka
• Android Auto: reba isomero ryawe, ibyo ukunda n'umurongo uri mu modoka
• Igikoresho cyo mu idirishya ryo mu rugo
• Ushobora gutangira gucuranga ako kanya iyo ecouteur cyangwa imodoka yawe ihujwe
• Equalizer ifite imiterere yateguwe mbere, insanganyamatsiko yoroheje n'iy'umwijima

Imiterere: FLAC, M4A, M4B (AAC idapfundikiye), MP3, OGG, Opus na WAV.

Ibyihariye hakurikijwe igishushanyo mbonera
Retro Player nta burenganzira bwo gukoresha interineti ifite, bityo nta kintu na kimwe gisohoka muri telefoni yawe: nta matangazo, isesengura, gukurikirana cyangwa konti. Nta burenganzira bwo kubika amakuru ikeneye.

Porogaramu y'ubuntu kandi ifunguye (GPL-3.0-cyangwa-iya nyuma). Ubuyobozi bw'umukoresha na kode y'isoko: https://github.com/r-e-t-r-o-p-l-a-y-e-r/retroplayer
