Ukukhululwa kokuqala kwi-F-Droid: ilayibrari yefolda enezintandokazi kunye ne-A–Z bar, ikhumbula indawo yokudlala kunye nesantya se-albhamu nganye okanye incwadi, izahluko ze-M4B, umqolo kunye noluhlu lwadlalwayo lwe-M3U, i-Android Auto, iwijethi yesikrini sasekhaya, ukhetho lwee-headphone, i-equalizer kunye neelwimi ezingama-47. Iimpawu ze-app azitshintshanga ukusukela kwi-0.1.0; yindlela kuphela ehlanganiswe ngayo etshintshileyo, ukuze i-F-Droid ikwazi ukuyiqinisekisa.
