Retro Player idlala umculo kunye neencwadi zeaudio ngqo kwiifolda ezikwifowuni yakho. Isebenza ngaphandle kweintanethi, ayinazintengiso kwaye ayifuni akhawunti.

Kutheni Retro Player?
• Iqhubeka kanye apho ubuyeke khona: yonke i-albhamu, incwadi yesandi kunye noluhlu lokudlala lugcina indawo yalo kunye nesantya salo, nokuba sele iqalile kwakhona.
• Akukho kuskena kwethala leencwadi: akukho index yokwakha kwaye akukho nto yokulinda. Yongeza ifolda uze uqale ukumamela; usetyenziso lufunda ifolda kuphela xa uyivula.
• Iifayile zakho azichukunyiswa: usetyenziso lubona kuphela iifolda ozikhethayo kwaye alutshintshi, alushukumisi okanye lucime nantoni na. Iifayile ezibhalayo kuphela ziluhlu lwadlalwayo olugcinayo.
• Uthetha ulwimi lwakho: iilwimi ezingaphezu kwe-100, ukusuka kwisiArabhu, isiHindi nesiSwahili ukuya kwisi-Indonesia, isiVietnamese nesiZulu.

Yenzelwe iincwadi zeaudio kunye nee-albhamu
• Isantya sokudlala ukusuka kwi-0.5× ukuya kwi-2.0×, sigciniwe ngento nganye; ukutsiba kwemizuzwana eli-15
• Izahluko ze-M4B kunye ne-M4A kwibha yokufuna, kuluhlu lwezahluko nakwisaziso
• Ibuyela umva imizuzwana embalwa xa uqhubeka emva kwekhefu elide
• Ukudlala okungenazikhewu; ii-albhamu ezinamadiski amaninzi zidlala ngokulandelelana kweediski

Iifolda zakho, iodolo yakho
• Khangela njengomphathi wefayile, ngee-breadcrumbs, ii-favorites kunye ne-A–Z bar
• Swayipha ukuze wongeze iifolda, ii-albhamu okanye iingoma kumgca kwaye uzigcine njengoluhlu lokudlala lwe-M3U
• Idlala uluhlu lwadlalwayo osele unalo
• Hlela i-albhamu nganye ngenombolo yengoma, igama lefayile okanye isihloko

Naphi na apho umamela khona
• Ulawulo kwisaziso, kwisikrini sokutshixa (ngokutsiba kwemizuzwana eli-15) nakwi-Bluetooth okanye amaqhosha emoto
• Android Auto: khangela ilayibrari yakho, izinto ozithandayo kunye nomgca emotweni
• Iwijethi yesikrini sasekhaya
• Ungakhetha ukuqala ukudlala kwangoko xa ii-headphones okanye imoto yakho iqhagamshelwe
• Isilinganisi esinee-presets, umxholo okhanyayo nomnyama

Iifomathi: FLAC, M4A, M4B (i-AAC engaguqulelwanga kwi-intanethi), MP3, OGG, Opus kunye neWAV.

Yabucala ngokwedizayini
I-Retro Player ayinayo imvume ye-intanethi, ngoko ke akukho nto iphuma kwifowuni yakho: akukho zintengiso, uhlalutyo, ukulandelela okanye iiakhawunti. Ayidingi mvume yokugcina.
