I-Retro Player idlala umculo kunye neencwadi zeaudio ngqo kwiifolda ezikwifowuni yakho. Isebenza ngaphandle kweintanethi, ayinazo iintengiso kwaye ayidingi akhawunti.

Kutheni i-Retro Player?
• Iqhubeka kanye apho ubuyeke khona: yonke i-albhamu, incwadi yeaudio kunye noluhlu lokudlala lugcina indawo kunye nesantya salo, nokuba emva kokuqalisa kwakhona.

• Akukho scan yelayibrari: akukho index yokwakha kwaye akukho nto yokulinda. Yongeza ifolda kwaye uqale ukumamela; usetyenziso lufunda ifolda kuphela xa uyivula.

• Iifayile zakho azichukunyiswa: usetyenziso lubona kuphela iifolda ozikhethayo kwaye alutshintshi, alushukumisi okanye lucime nantoni na. Iifayile ezibhalayo kuphela ziluhlu lokudlala olugcinayo.
• Ithetha ulwimi lwakho: iilwimi ezingaphezu kwe-100, ukusuka kwisiArabhu, isiHindi nesiSwahili ukuya kwisi-Indonesia, isiVietnam kunye nesiZulu.

Yenzelwe iincwadi zeaudio kunye nee-albhamu
• Isantya sokudlala ukusuka kwi-0.5× ukuya kwi-2.0×, sigcinwe ngento nganye; Ukutsiba kwemizuzwana eli-15
• Izahluko ze-M4B kunye ne-M4A kwibha yokufuna, kuluhlu lwezahluko nakwisaziso
• Ibuyela umva imizuzwana embalwa xa uqhubeka emva kwekhefu elide
• Ukudlala okungenazikhewu; ii-albhamu zediski ezininzi zidlala ngokulandelelana kwediski

Iifolda zakho, iodolo yakho
• Khangela njengomphathi wefayile, ngee-breadcrumbs, ii-favorites kunye ne-A–Z bar
• Swayipha ukuze wongeze iifolda, ii-albhamu okanye iingoma kumgca kwaye uyigcine njengoluhlu lokudlala lwe-M3U
• Idlala uluhlu lokudlala olusele unalo
• Hlela i-albhamu nganye ngenombolo yengoma, igama lefayile okanye isihloko

Naphi na apho umamela khona
• Ilawula kwisaziso, kwisikrini sokutshixa (ngotsiba lwemizuzwana eli-15) nakwi-Bluetooth okanye amaqhosha emoto
• I-Android Auto: khangela ilayibrari yakho, ii-favorites kunye nomgca emotweni
• Iwijethi yesikrini sasekhaya
• Iqala ukudlala ngokukhawuleza xa ii-headphones okanye imoto yakho iqhagamshele
• Isilinganisi esine-presets, umxholo okhanyayo nomnyama

Iifomathi: I-FLAC, i-M4A, i-M4B (i-AAC engabhaliswanga), i-MP3, i-OGG, i-Opus kunye ne-WAV.

Yabucala ngokoyilo
I-Retro Player ayinayo imvume ye-intanethi, ngoko akukho nto iphuma kwifowuni yakho: akukho ntengiso, uhlalutyo, ukulandelela okanye iiakhawunti. Ayidingi mvume yokugcina.
