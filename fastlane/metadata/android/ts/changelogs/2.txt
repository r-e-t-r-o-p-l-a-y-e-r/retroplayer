Nkandziyiso wo sungula eka F-Droid: layiburari ya tifolda leyi nga na leswi tsakeriwaka na baari ya A–Z, yi tsundzuka ndhawu yo tlanga na rivilo ra alibamu yin’wana na yin’wana kumbe buku, tindzima ta M4B, minxaxamelo ya ku tlanga ya layini na M3U, Android Auto, swihlawulekisi swa ti-headphone, equalizer na tindzimi ta 47. Swihlawulekisi swa app a swi cincanga ku sukela eka 0.1.0; ntsena ndlela leyi yi hlengeletiweke ha yona yi cinciwile, leswaku F-Droid yi ta kota ku yi tiyisisa.
