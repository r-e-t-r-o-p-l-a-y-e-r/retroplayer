Retro Player plays music and audiobooks straight from the folders on your phone. It works offline, has no ads and needs no account.

Why Retro Player?
• Continues exactly where you left off: every album, audiobook and playlist keeps its own position and speed, even after a restart.
• No library scan: there is no index to build and nothing to wait for. Add a folder and start listening; the app reads a folder only when you open it.
• Your files stay untouched: the app sees only the folders you pick and never changes, moves or deletes anything. The only files it writes are playlists you save.
• Speaks your language: more than 100 languages, from Arabic, Hindi and Swahili to Indonesian, Vietnamese and Zulu.

Made for audiobooks and albums
• Playback speed from 0.5× to 2.0×, saved per item; 15-second jumps
• M4B and M4A chapters on the seek bar, in a chapter list and in the notification
• Rewinds a few seconds when you continue after a longer break
• Gapless playback; multi-disc albums play in disc order

Your folders, your order
• Browse like a file manager, with breadcrumbs, favorites and an A–Z bar
• Swipe to add folders, albums or tracks to the queue and save it as an M3U playlist
• Plays the playlists you already have
• Sort each album by track number, file name or title

Wherever you listen
• Controls in the notification, on the lock screen (with 15-second jumps) and on Bluetooth or car buttons
• Android Auto: browse your library, favorites and queue in the car
• Home-screen widget
• Optionally starts playing as soon as headphones or your car connect
• Equalizer with presets, light and dark theme

Formats: FLAC, M4A, M4B (unencrypted AAC), MP3, OGG, Opus and WAV.

Private by design
Retro Player has no Internet permission, so nothing leaves your phone: no ads, analytics, tracking or accounts. It needs no storage permission either.

Free and open source software (GPL-3.0-or-later). User guide and source code: https://github.com/r-e-t-r-o-p-l-a-y-e-r/retroplayer
