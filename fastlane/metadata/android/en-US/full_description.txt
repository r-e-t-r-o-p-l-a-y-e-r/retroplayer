Retro Player plays music and audiobooks from folders on your phone. Pick a folder and the app shows it as it is on disk: folders, albums and playlists. No account, no ads and no network access.

Made for audiobooks and albums
• Resumes every album, book and playlist where you stopped
• Playback speed from 0.5× to 2.0×, saved per item; 15-second jumps
• M4B and M4A chapters on the seek bar, in a chapter list and in the notification
• Rewinds a few seconds when you continue after a longer break
• Gapless playback; multi-disc albums play in disc order

Your folders, your order
• Browse like a file manager, with breadcrumbs, favorites and an A–Z bar
• Swipe to add folders, albums or tracks to the queue and save it as an M3U playlist
• Plays the playlists you already have
• Sort each album by track number, file name or title

Wherever you listen
• Controls in the notification, on the lock screen and on Bluetooth or car buttons
• Android Auto: browse your library, favorites and queue in the car
• Home-screen widget
• Optionally continues when your headphones reconnect
• Equalizer with presets, light and dark theme, 47 languages

Formats: MP3, M4A, M4B (unencrypted AAC), FLAC, WAV, OGG and Opus.

Private by design
Retro Player has no Internet permission, so nothing leaves your phone: no ads, analytics, tracking or accounts. It needs no storage permission either. It reads only the folders you pick and never changes your files; the only files it writes are playlists you save.

Free and open source software (GPL-3.0-or-later). User guide and source code: https://github.com/r-e-t-r-o-p-l-a-y-e-r/retroplayer
