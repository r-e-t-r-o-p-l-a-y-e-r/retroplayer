First release on F-Droid: folder library with favorites and A–Z bar, remembers the playback position and speed for each album or book, M4B chapters, queue and M3U playlists, Android Auto, home-screen widget, headphone options, equalizer and 47 languages. The app's features are unchanged since 0.1.0; only the way it is compiled changed, so that F-Droid can verify it.
