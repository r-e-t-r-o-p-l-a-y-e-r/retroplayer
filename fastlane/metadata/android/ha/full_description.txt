Retro Player yana kunna kiɗa da littattafan sauti kai tsaye daga manyan fayilolin wayarka. Yana aiki a layi, ba shi da talla kuma baya buƙatar asusu.

Me yasa Retro Player?
• Yana ci gaba daidai inda kuka tsaya: kowane kundin waƙoƙi, littafin sauti da jerin waƙoƙi yana kiyaye matsayinsa da saurinsa, koda bayan sake kunnawa.
• Babu duba ɗakin karatu: babu wani fihirisa da za a gina kuma babu abin da za a jira. Ƙara babban fayil kuma fara sauraro; app ɗin yana karanta babban fayil ne kawai lokacin da kuka buɗe shi.
• Fayilolinku ba sa taɓawa: app ɗin yana ganin manyan fayilolin da kuka zaɓa kawai kuma ba ya canzawa, motsa ko goge komai. Fayilolin da yake rubutawa sune jerin waƙoƙin da kuka adana.
• Yana jin yarenku: sama da harsuna 100, daga Larabci, Hindi da Swahili zuwa Indonesian, Vietnamese da Zulu.

An yi shi don littattafan sauti da kundin waƙoƙi
• Saurin sake kunnawa daga 0.5× zuwa 2.0×, an adana kowane abu; Tsalle-tsalle na daƙiƙa 15
• Surorin M4B da M4A akan sandar nema, a cikin jerin babi da kuma a cikin sanarwar
• Yana sake dawowa daƙiƙa kaɗan lokacin da ka ci gaba bayan hutu mai tsawo
• Kunnawa mara iyaka; kundin faifai da yawa suna kunnawa cikin tsari na diski

Foldulolinka, odar ka
• Yi bincike kamar mai sarrafa fayil, tare da gurasar burodi, abubuwan da aka fi so da sandar A–Z
• Danna don ƙara manyan fayiloli, kundin waƙoƙi ko waƙoƙi zuwa layi kuma adana su azaman jerin waƙoƙin M3U
• Yana kunna jerin waƙoƙin da ka riga ka mallaka
• Rarraba kowane kundin waƙoƙi ta lambar waƙa, sunan fayil ko take

Duk inda ka saurara
• Sarrafa a cikin sanarwar, akan allon kulle (tare da tsalle na daƙiƙa 15) da kuma akan maɓallan Bluetooth ko mota
• Android Auto: bincika ɗakin karatu, abubuwan da aka fi so da layi a cikin mota
• Na'urar widget ta allo ta gida
• Zaɓin zai fara kunnawa da zarar belun kunne ko motarka sun haɗu
• Mai daidaitawa tare da saitattun saiti, jigo mai haske da duhu

Tsarin: FLAC, M4A, M4B (AAC mara ɓoyewa), MP3, OGG, Opus da WAV.

Na sirri ta hanyar ƙira
Retro Player ba shi da izinin Intanet, don haka babu abin da zai fita daga wayarka: babu talla, nazari, bin diddigi ko asusu. Ba ya buƙatar izinin ajiya.

Manhajar kyauta da buɗewa (GPL-3.0-ko-bayan haka). Jagorar mai amfani da lambar tushe: https://github.com/r-e-t-r-o-p-l-a-y-e-r/retroplayer
