Ukukhishwa kokuqala ku-F-Droid: umtapo wefolda onezintandokazi kanye nebha ye-A–Z, ukhumbula indawo yokudlala kanye nesivinini se-albhamu noma incwadi ngayinye, izahluko ze-M4B, umugqa kanye nohlu lwadlalwayo lwe-M3U, i-Android Auto, iwijethi yesikrini sasekhaya, izinketho zamahedfoni, isilinganisi kanye nezilimi ezingu-47. Izici zohlelo lokusebenza azikashintshi kusukela ku-0.1.0; indlela ehlanganiswe ngayo kuphela eshintshile, ukuze i-F-Droid ikwazi ukuyiqinisekisa.
