Retro Player idlala umculo nezincwadi ezilalelwayo ngqo kusuka kumafolda efonini yakho. Isebenza ungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi, ayinazikhangiso futhi ayidingi i-akhawunti.

Kungani Retro Player?
• Iqhubeka lapho ushiye khona: yonke i-albhamu, i-audiobook kanye nohlu lwadlalwayo lugcina indawo yalo kanye nesivinini, ngisho nangemva kokuqala kabusha.
• Akukho ukuskena komtapo wolwazi: akukho nkomba okufanele yakhiwe futhi akukho okumele kulindelwe. Engeza ifolda bese uqala ukulalela; uhlelo lokusebenza lufunda ifolda kuphela uma ulivula.
• Amafayela akho ahlala engathintekile: uhlelo lokusebenza lubona amafolda owakhethayo kuphela futhi alushintshi, alususi noma lususe noma yini. Amafayela oluwabhalayo kuphela uhlu lwadlalwayo olulondolozayo.
• Ukhuluma ulimi lwakho: izilimi ezingaphezu kwe-100, kusukela ku-Arabhu, isiHindi nesiSwahili kuya ku-Indonesian, isiVietnamese nesiZulu.

Kwenzelwe ama-audiobook nama-albhamu
• Isivinini sokudlala kusukela ku-0.5× kuya ku-2.0×, silondolozwe ngento ngayinye; ukugxuma kwemizuzwana eyi-15
• Izahluko ze-M4B kanye ne-M4A kubha yokufuna, ohlwini lwezahluko kanye nasesazisweni
• Ibuyela emuva imizuzwana embalwa uma uqhubeka ngemva kwekhefu elide
• Ukudlala okungenazikhala; ama-albhamu anama-disc amaningi adlala ngokulandelana kwama-disc

Amafolda akho, i-oda lakho
• Phequlula njengomphathi wefayela, ngezinkwa zesinkwa, izintandokazi kanye nebha ye-A–Z
• Swayipha ukuze ungeze amafolda, ama-albhamu noma amathrekhi kulayini bese uwugcina njengohlu lwadlalwayo lwe-M3U
• Idlala uhlu lwadlalwayo osuvele unalo
• Hlela i-albhamu ngayinye ngenombolo yethrekhi, igama lefayela noma isihloko

Nomaphi lapho ulalela khona
• Izilawuli kwisaziso, esikrinini sokukhiya (ngokugxuma kwemizuzwana eyi-15) kanye nezinkinobho ze-Bluetooth noma zemoto
• Android Auto: phequlula ilabhulali yakho, izintandokazi kanye nomugqa emotweni
• Iwijethi yesikrini sasekhaya
• Uma uthanda, uqala ukudlala ngokushesha nje lapho ama-headphone noma imoto yakho ixhuma
• Isilinganisi esinamasethingi ahleliwe, itimu ekhanyayo nemnyama

Amafomethi: I-FLAC, i-M4A, i-M4B (i-AAC engabethelwe), i-MP3, i-OGG, i-Opus kanye ne-WAV.

Kuyimfihlo ngomklamo
I-Retro Player ayinayo imvume ye-inthanethi, ngakho akukho lutho oluphuma efonini yakho: akukho zikhangiso, ukuhlaziya, ukulandelela noma ama-akhawunti. Ayidingi imvume yokugcina futhi.
