I-Retro Player idlala umculo nama-audiobook ngqo kusuka kumafolda efonini yakho. Isebenza ungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi, ayinazikhangiso futhi ayidingi i-akhawunti.

Kungani i-Retro Player?
• Iqhubeka lapho ugcine khona: yonke i-albhamu, i-audiobook kanye nohlu lwadlalwayo lugcina indawo yalo kanye nesivinini, ngisho nangemva kokuqala kabusha.

• Akukho ukuskena komtapo wolwazi: akukho index yokwakha futhi akukho okumele ulinde. Engeza ifolda bese uqala ukulalela; uhlelo lokusebenza lufunda ifolda kuphela uma ulivula.

• Amafayela akho ahlala engathintekile: uhlelo lokusebenza lubona amafolda owakhethayo kuphela futhi alushintshi, luhambisa noma lususe noma yini. Amafayela kuphela eliwabhalayo uhlu lwadlalwayo olugcinayo.
• Ikhuluma ulimi lwakho: izilimi ezingaphezu kwe-100, kusukela ku-Arabhu, isiHindi nesiSwahili kuya ku-Indonesian, isiVietnam nesiZulu.

Yenzelwe ama-audiobook nama-albhamu
• Isivinini sokudlala kusukela ku-0.5× kuya ku-2.0×, silondolozwe ngento ngayinye; Ukugxuma kwemizuzwana eyi-15
• Izahluko ze-M4B kanye ne-M4A kubha yokufuna, ohlwini lwezahluko kanye nesaziso
• Ibuyela emuva imizuzwana embalwa uma uqhubeka ngemuva kwekhefu elide
• Ukudlala okungenazikhala; ama-albhamu ama-multi-disc adlala ngokulandelana kwediski

Amafolda akho, i-oda lakho
• Phequlula njengomphathi wefayela, ngezinkwa zesinkwa, izintandokazi kanye nebha ye-A–Z
• Swayipha ukuze ungeze amafolda, ama-albhamu noma amathrekhi kulayini bese uwugcina njengohlu lokudlala lwe-M3U
• Idlala uhlu lokudlalayo osuvele unalo
• Hlela i-albhamu ngayinye ngenombolo yethrekhi, igama lefayela noma isihloko

Noma yikuphi lapho ulalela khona
• Ilawula isaziso, esikrinini sokukhiya (ngokugxuma kwemizuzwana eyi-15) kanye nezinkinobho ze-Bluetooth noma zemoto
• I-Android Auto: phequlula umtapo wakho wezincwadi, izintandokazi kanye nomugqa emotweni
• Iwijethi yesikrini sasekhaya
• Iqala ukudlala ngokuzithandela ngokushesha nje lapho ama-headphone noma imoto yakho ixhuma
• Isilinganisi esinama-preset, itimu ekhanyayo nemnyama

Amafomethi: I-FLAC, i-M4A, i-M4B (i-AAC engabethelwe), i-MP3, i-OGG, i-Opus kanye ne-WAV.

Iyimfihlo ngomklamo
I-Retro Player ayinayo imvume ye-inthanethi, ngakho-ke akukho lutho oluphuma efonini yakho: akukho zikhangiso, ukuhlaziya, ukulandelela noma ama-akhawunti. Ayidingi imvume yokugcina futhi.
