Retro Player plays audio from folders you choose on your phone or computer. It suits music collections and audiobooks: gapless albums, saved playback position, variable speed, and background playback with lock-screen controls.

What you can do
• Add library folders with the system picker — your files stay where they are
• Browse folders on disk (breadcrumbs and favorites); open grouped albums for track lists; build and play playlists
• Resume where you stopped; multi-disc albums in one folder play in disc order
• Control playback from notifications and Bluetooth / car media buttons
• Change theme, language, and equalizer presets

Supported formats
MP3, FLAC, WAV, and unencrypted AAC (.m4a / .m4b) — plus OGG on Android and Linux.

Privacy by design
Release builds do not request Internet access: no streaming, ads, analytics, crash trackers, or in-app purchases. The app only reads folders you explicitly grant.

Step-by-step help with screenshots is in the project user guide (doc/USAGE.md on the public source repository).
